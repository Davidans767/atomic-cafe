# Facebook Post Template — Atomic Cafe

## Format
- Image: 1200x630px (landscape) or 1080x1080 (square)
- Caption: 80-150 words ideal (longer than Instagram, FB rewards reading time)
- Hashtags: 3-5 max (FB is not Instagram, don't overdo it)

## Post Structure

```
[Hook line — emoji + bold statement]
[שורת ריווח]
[סיפור / ערך — 2-3 משפטים]
[שורת ריווח]
[CTA — מה אנחנו רוצים שיעשו]
[שורת ריווח]
[פרטים פרקטיים: כתובת, שעות, טלפון]
[שורת ריווח]
#hashtags
```

| רביעי | המלצת לקוח / UGC | social proof |
| חמישי | אירוע סוף שבוע | reach |

## Image Style
- Always natural light, never flash
- Top-down for food, eye-level for drinks
- Include a hand/person when possible (humans = clicks)
- Brand logo bottom-left, small (60px), white on dark or ink on light

## Boosting Rules
- Boost only posts with >5% organic engagement after 24h
- Budget: ₪50-100 per boost, 3-day run
- Target: locals 5km radius, ages 25-55
