ATOMIC CAFE — ANNUAL FOOD SAFETY TRAINING NOTES
=================================================
Last updated: January 2026
Next review due: January 2027
Regulatory basis: Israeli Ministry of Health food business regulations

1. PERSONAL HYGIENE
   - Wash hands: before shift, after restroom, after touching face/hair/phone,
     after handling garbage, between tasks
   - Hand washing procedure: wet, soap 20 sec, scrub under nails, rinse, dry
     with disposable towel
   - No jewelry on hands/wrists while handling food (wedding band exception
     with glove over it)
   - Hair tied back and covered (cap or net provided)
   - Clean uniform daily — no outside clothing behind counter
   - Report illness: vomiting, diarrhea, jaundice, fever — DO NOT come to work.
     Notify shift manager immediately. Return only with doctor clearance.

2. TEMPERATURE CONTROL
   - Cold storage: fridge at 1-4°C, freezer at -18°C or below
   - Check fridge/freezer temps at opening and closing — log in temp sheet
   - Milk must be refrigerated immediately after delivery
   - Dairy products: discard if left above 5°C for more than 2 hours
   - Pastries with cream/cheese filling: display max 4 hours at room temp,
     then discard
   - Hot food (soup, quiche): hold above 60°C or cool below 5°C within 2 hours

3. CROSS-CONTAMINATION
   - Separate cutting boards: green = vegetables, red = raw meat (rare but
     for kitchen prep), white = dairy/bread
   - Never store raw items above ready-to-eat items in fridge
   - Sanitize surfaces between different food types
   - Allergen tools: dedicated pitcher for nut-free milks, separate blender
     for smoothies

4. ALLERGEN MANAGEMENT
   - Eight major allergens posted in kitchen: milk, eggs, peanuts, tree nuts,
     wheat, soy, fish, shellfish
   - Menu allergen matrix available at POS — check before confirming any order
     flagged by customer
   - "I'm not sure" is never acceptable — check the matrix or ask the manager
   - Nut-free drinks: use dedicated pitcher, wipe steam wand, fresh cloth

5. CLEANING & SANITIZATION
   - Three-step: wash with soap → rinse → sanitize (food-safe solution)
   - Sanitizer concentration: check with test strips (bucket by dish station)
   - Espresso group heads: backflush with cleaner every close
   - Steam wand: wipe + purge after EVERY use
   - Surfaces: sanitize every 2 hours during service, immediately after spills
   - Floor: sweep hourly, mop at close with designated solution
   - Garbage: remove when 3/4 full, never compress by hand, wash hands after

6. RECEIVING & STORAGE
   - Inspect all deliveries: check temp (cold items), expiry dates, packaging
     integrity
   - Reject: dented cans, torn packaging, items above safe temp, expired items
   - FIFO (First In First Out): new stock behind old stock, always
   - Label all open containers: item name + date opened
   - Dry storage: off the floor (min 15 cm), away from walls, below chemicals

7. PEST CONTROL
   - Keep doors closed; screen on back door
   - No food debris on floors, counters, or storage areas
   - Report any signs: droppings, gnaw marks, insects — to manager immediately
   - Monthly pest control service (Terminix) — do not move bait stations

8. DOCUMENTATION
   - Temperature log: filled twice daily (opening + closing)
   - Cleaning log: initialed after each scheduled task
   - Incident log: any food safety issue, customer complaint about food,
     or delivery rejection
   - Keep logs for 12 months minimum

9. EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
   - Customer allergic reaction: call Magen David Adom (101), keep customer
     calm, note what was consumed
   - Fire in kitchen: turn off gas, use extinguisher (class K for grease),
     evacuate if needed, call 102
   - Water contamination notice: stop all water-based drinks, switch to
     bottled water, notify manager
